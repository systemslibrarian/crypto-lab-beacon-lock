Beacon Lock

Timelock encryption · drand beacon · IBE-based release

Lock a message to a round that has not happened yet, then watch the beacon's signature on that round arrive and act as the decryption key — real BLS12-381 pairings, and a decryptor that refuses every other round.

WHY IT MATTERS

Sealed bids, embargoed disclosures and dead-man switches all need a secret that opens on a date rather than on request — and every escrow you trust to hold it is an escrow that can be subpoenaed, breached or bribed. A beacon lock removes the custodian: nobody holds the key early, and no amount of hardware buys its way in.
