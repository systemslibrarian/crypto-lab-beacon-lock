NEW DEMO BRIEF
- Repo name:         crypto-lab-beacon-lock
- Short name (H1):   Beacon Lock
- Subtitle:          Timelock encryption · drand beacon · IBE-based release
- One-liner:         Beacon-based timelock encryption where a ciphertext is locked to a future round of a public randomness beacon — when the beacon publishes that round's signature, it becomes the decryption key. Nobody grinds, everybody waits.
- Concept to teach:  Three ways to delay a secret: make someone grind (time-lock puzzle), make everyone grind the same chain (VDF), or make everyone wait for a public event (beacon timelock). Beacon timelock is IBE in disguise — the "identity" is the future round number, and the beacon's signature on that round is the IBE private key extraction. No computation race, just patience.
- Primitives/spec:   Rabin 1983 (timed-release crypto concept), Drand beacon (League of Entropy), BLS12-381 threshold signatures, Boneh-Franklin IBE as the release mechanism
- Accent (--accent): #f59e0b
- Favicon emoji:     ⏳
- In scope:          Encrypt a message to a future round number; show the IBE structure — round number as identity, beacon public key as IBE master public key; simulated beacon: a timer ticks rounds, each round produces a BLS signature; when the target round arrives, the signature is used as the IBE private key to decrypt; three-model comparison: time-lock puzzle (grind to open), VDF (verify fast, compute slow), beacon lock (wait for external event); "what if the beacon dies?" — show that the ciphertext becomes permanently locked; real BLS12-381 via @noble/curves; link to VDF demo and time-lock puzzle demo; composability with IBE Gate
- Non-goals:         No real drand network queries (simulated beacon for offline use); no witness encryption (explicitly distinct — this is IBE-based, not NP-statement-based); no threshold key generation ceremony; beacon is simulated as a local timer, not a live network feed — state this
